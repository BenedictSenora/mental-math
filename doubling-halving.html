The Doubling & Halving Trick

Core Arithmetic Module 6 of 6

Transforming Hard Multiplication

In multiplication, if you double one factor and cut the other factor in half, the final product stays completely unchanged.

Formula: A \times B = (2A) \times (B \div 2).

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Spot an awkward number ending in 5 (like 15, 25, or 35) paired with an even number.
  2. Double the number ending in 5 to turn it into a clean multiple of 10.
  3. Cut the even number in half, then multiply the two new easy numbers together.

Tutorial 1: Single Step Doubling & Halving

Use one round of doubling and halving to eliminate messy 5s immediately.

Example: 16 × 35

1. Double 35 → 70.

2. Halve 16 → 8.

3. Multiply the easy numbers: 8 × 70 = 560.

Tutorial 2: Double-Step Transformation

For numbers ending in 25 or 75, repeat the trick twice to turn them into 100 or 300.

Example: 24 × 25

1. First step: 12 × 50.

2. Second step: 6 × 100 = 600.
