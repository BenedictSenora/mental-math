Multiplying by 5: Halve & Shift

Core Arithmetic Module 4 of 6

Why Multiplying by 5 Is Easy

Since 5 = \frac{10}{2}, multiplying any number by 5 is the exact same thing as cutting the number in half and multiplying by 10.

Formula: N \times 5 = (N \div 2) \times 10.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Take the number you want to multiply by 5.
  2. Divide that number in half (by 2).
  3. Multiply by 10 (attach a 0 to the end, or drop the decimal point if it ends in .5).

Tutorial 1: Even Numbers

When multiplying an even number by 5, cut it in half cleanly and tack a zero onto the right.

Example: 48 × 5

1. Cut 48 in half: 48 ÷ 2 = 24.

2. Add a zero to the end: 240.

Tutorial 2: Odd Numbers

When multiplying an odd number by 5, subtract 1, cut it in half, and attach a 5 to the end instead of a 0.

Example: 67 × 5

1. Subtract 1 from 67 to get 66, then halve it: 66 ÷ 2 = 33.

2. Attach a 5 to the end: 335.
