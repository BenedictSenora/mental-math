Multiplying by 11: The Split & Add Trick

Core Arithmetic Module 3 of 6

Why Multiplying by 11 Is Instant

You don't need to multiply at all to multiply any two-digit number by 11—it is purely a visual pattern of splitting digits and adding them together.

Pattern Formula: For a two-digit number AB, the result is A \mid (A+B) \mid B.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Separate the two digits of the number to leave a blank space in the middle.
  2. Add the two digits together in your head.
  3. Place the sum in the middle space (carry the 1 to the left digit if the sum is 10 or more).

Tutorial 1: Standard Split (No Carry)

When the two digits add up to 9 or less, simply drop their sum right between them.

Example: 35 × 11

1. Split 3 and 5: 3 _ 5.

2. Add digits: 3 + 5 = 8.

3. Insert in middle: 385.

Tutorial 2: Split with a Carry (Sum ≥ 10)

If the two digits add up to 10 or higher, keep the ones digit in the middle and add 1 to the first digit.

Example: 76 × 11

1. Split 7 and 6: 7 _ 6.

2. Add digits: 7 + 6 = 13.

3. Put 3 in the middle and add 1 to 7: 836.
