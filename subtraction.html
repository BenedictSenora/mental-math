Left-to-Right Subtraction

Core Arithmetic Module 2 of 6

Why Traditional Subtraction Is Slow

Borrowing and carrying from right to left forces you to cross out numbers and hold messy remainders in your head.

Speed math simplifies subtraction: work from left to right or round up to clean numbers and add back the difference.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Split the number being subtracted into tens and ones.
  2. Subtract the tens first from your starting number.
  3. Subtract the remaining ones to get your final answer.

Tutorial 1: Left-to-Right Place Splitting

Break the second number down into clean place values and subtract step-by-step from largest to smallest.

Example: 84 - 36

1. Split 36 into 30 + 6.

2. Subtract tens: 84 - 30 = 54.

3. Subtract ones: 54 - 6 = 48.

Tutorial 2: The Rounding & Add-Back Method

When subtracting a number ending in 7, 8, or 9, round it up to the nearest ten, subtract, and add back the extra amount.

Example: 92 - 48

1. Treat 48 as 50 (rounded up by 2).

2. Subtract: 92 - 50 = 42.

3. Add back the extra 2: 42 + 2 = 44.
