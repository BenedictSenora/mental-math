The Box Method for Large Numbers

Advanced Multiplication Module 3 of 4

Mental Grid Framework

Break large numbers apart into tens and units mentally, visualize a 2x2 grid box, multiply the easy parts separately, and add them up.

Formula: (A + B) \times (C + D) = AC + AD + BC + BD.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Deconstruct both numbers into tens and ones (e.g., 24 becomes 20 + 4).
  2. Calculate four partial products in your head (Tens×Tens, Tens×Ones, etc.).
  3. Add all four products together to find the grand total.

Tutorial 1: Standard 2-Digit Grid

Visualize breaking down both factors into expanded form before summing the cross products.

Example: 24 × 13

1. Split into (20 + 4) and (10 + 3).

2. Cross products: 20\times10 = 200 | 20\times3 = 60 | 4\times10 = 40 | 4\times3 = 12.

3. Add total: 200 + 60 + 40 + 12 = {312}.

Tutorial 2: Tens & Units Distribution

An alternative workflow: multiply the first whole number by the tens part, then by the ones part.

Example: 32 × 14

1. Multiply by ten: 32 \times 10 = 320.

2. Multiply by four: 32 \times 4 = 128.

3. Add parts: 320 + 128 = {448}.
