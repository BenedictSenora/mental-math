Daily Math Practice Routine

Practice & Memory Module 3 of 3

Habit Stacking & Spaced Repetition Framework

True calculation fluency is built through a sustainable, 10-minute daily micro-routine rather than stressful marathon cram sessions.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Minutes 1–3 (Warm-up): Fast addition & subtraction drills (left-to-right).
  2. Minutes 4–7 (Core Focus): Practice one specific mental math trick per day.
  3. Minutes 8–10 (Application): Estimate a mock grocery bill or restaurant tip.

Tutorial 1: Structuring a 10-Minute Micro-Drill

Break your daily session into three rapid, high-focus compartments.

Example Session Breakdown

1. Warm-up: 3 minutes of random 2-digit addition.

2. Core skill: 4 minutes squaring numbers ending in 5.

3. Application: 3 minutes calculating tip rates.

Tutorial 2: Weekly Skill Rotation

Rotate categories across the week to prevent cognitive burnout and ensure comprehensive retention.

Example Weekly Calendar

Monday: Core Arithmetic | Wednesday: Advanced Multiplication | Friday: Real-World Apps.
