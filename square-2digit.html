Squaring Any 2-Digit Number

Advanced Multiplication Module 2 of 4

The Algebraic Identity Framework

Squaring numbers close to clean base numbers (like 50 or 100) uses the difference-of-squares concept: a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b).

Pattern: Anchor your calculation around the distance (d) from the base value of 50.

3-Step Base-50 Tutorial

  1. Find the distance (d) your number is from 50.
  2. Add or subtract d from 25 to get your first two digits.
  3. Square the distance (d^2) and place it at the end (use two digits, e.g., 9 becomes 09).

Tutorial 1: Numbers Above 50

Add the distance to 25 for numbers greater than 50.

Example: 53²

1. Distance from 50 is d = 3.

2. Add to 25: 25 + 3 = 28.

3. Square distance: 3^2 = 09 → Answer: 2,809.

Tutorial 2: Numbers Below 50

Subtract the distance from 25 for numbers less than 50.

Example: 48²

1. Distance from 50 is d = 2 (below).

2. Subtract from 25: 25 - 2 = 23.

3. Square distance: 2^2 = 04 → Answer: 2,304.
