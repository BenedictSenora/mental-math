The 10% Base Multiplier Concept

Calculate standard restaurant tips (15% or 20%) in seconds without reaching for a phone calculator by anchoring off a clean 10% base.

Formula: \text{20\% Tip} = (\text{Bill} \times 0.10) \times 2.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Round the bill to the nearest whole dollar for speed.
  2. Move the decimal point one place left to find 10%.
  3. Double it for a 20% tip, or add half of it for a 15% tip.

Tutorial 1: Calculating a 20% Tip

Move the decimal point left once, then double the resulting number.

Example: 20% Tip on a $45.00 Bill

1. Find 10% by shifting decimal: $4.50.

2. Double it: $4.50 × 2 = $9.00 tip.

Tutorial 2: Calculating a 15% Tip

Take your 10% number and add half of that value to it.

Example: 15% Tip on a $60.00 Bill

1. Find 10%: $6.00.

2. Find half of 10%: $3.00.

3. Add together: $6.00 + $3.00 = $9.00 tip.
