Multiplying by 9: The Hand Method

Advanced Multiplication Module 1 of 4

The Concept of Finger Math

You can use your ten fingers as an instant calculator for any single-digit number multiplied by 9—no memorization required.

Pattern Formula: Fingers to the left represent tens; fingers to the right represent ones.

3-Step Tutorial

  1. Hold both hands out in front of you with fingers spread straight.
  2. Count from left to right and curl down the finger matching your multiplier.
  3. Count the fingers to the left (tens) and fingers to the right (ones).

Tutorial 1: Finger Curl Method

A tactile visual pattern that makes 9s multiplication foolproof.

Example: 9 × 4

1. Curl down the 4th finger from the left.

2. Fingers to the left = 3 (tens).

3. Fingers to the right = 6 (ones) → Answer: 36.

Tutorial 2: The Tens-Complement Rule

Algebraically, multiplying by 9 is the same as multiplying by 10 and subtracting the original number.

Example: 7 × 9

1. Multiply by 10: 7 × 10 = 70.

2. Subtract the number itself: 70 - 7 = 63.
